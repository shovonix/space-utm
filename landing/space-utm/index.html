How it works

Three steps. No account.

1Point

Add your link

Paste where it goes and a short code for the video, like fp. Today’s date is added for you.

2Pick

Choose placements

Description, pinned comment, Shorts, bios, newsletter, podcast notes, or your own.

3Copy

Paste them in

Copy one link or all of them, download a CSV, or grab a QR code for the video.

Features

Small, sharp, and built for the job.

Every placement at once

One video gets a link for each place you post it, so you can tell the description from the pinned comment.

Short, dated campaigns

Campaigns are a short code plus the date, like fp220726. Change the date to rebuild an old link exactly.

Ready for GA4

YouTube video placements land in Organic Video, social in Organic Social, newsletter in Email.

QR codes for video

Black on white, or white on transparent to drop straight onto your edit.

Catches mistakes

Lowercases names so reports don’t split, replaces old UTM tags, and warns about names GA4 misfiles.

No redirects

Links point straight at your site. Nothing breaks if this site goes away.

Always free

No plans. No trial. No catch.

Space UTM is free to use, and it stays free. No sign-up, no link shortener, and your links are saved only on your device.
